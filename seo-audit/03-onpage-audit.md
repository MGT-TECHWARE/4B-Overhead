**Updated:** 2026-08-02

Covers the on-page layer across all 36 generated pages. `02-technical-onpage-audit.md`
holds the technical/crawl half; this file is titles, headings, body structure,
linking, and images.

## Title tag rules applied

- Keyword first, brand last, separated by `|` or `—`
- Target ≤ 60–65 visible characters so Google doesn't truncate
- Geo qualifier ("West & North TX", "Texas") on commercial-intent pages
- City pages: `cityTitle()` in `src/seo/jsonld.ts` composes them from the city
  record so 15 titles can't drift apart by hand

**Spot check:**

| Page | Title | Chars |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Garage Door Repair & Install — West & North TX \| 4B Overhead | 61 |
| Spring repair | Garage Door Spring Repair (Emergency) \| 4B Overhead Doors | 57 |
| Commercial | Commercial Overhead Doors — Texas \| 4B Overhead Doors | 53 |
| About | About 4B Overhead Doors — Colten Beaty, Owner | 45 |

⚠️ Two blog `metaTitle`s run slightly long once the brand suffix is counted.
Not a defect — Google truncates the tail, which is the brand, and the keyword
survives. Left alone deliberately.

---

## Meta description rules applied

- 120–170 characters
- Lead with what the service is, then where, then a differentiator
- Phone number included on commercial-intent pages (it renders as a tappable
  CTA on mobile SERPs)
- Never duplicated across pages — city descriptions are composed per-city by
  `cityDescription()`

---

## H1 rules

- Exactly one `<h1>` per page — verified across all 36
- Eyebrow labels are `<p>`, not headings. This was the single biggest on-page
  defect in the original baseline (7 × `<h3>` sitting above `<h2>`), fixed in
  the first pass and not regressed since — new pages follow the same pattern.
- Heading order is strictly descending; no skipped levels

---

## First 100 words (BLUF)

Every commercial-intent page opens with a complete factual sentence naming the
business, the service, and the region — because that opening chunk is what AI
engines retrieve and quote. Brand-voice taglines are not citable.

Example — `/services/garage-door-spring-repair`:

> "A broken garage door spring is the most common way a door goes down, and the
> one repair you should not attempt yourself. 4B Overhead Doors replaces torsion
> and extension springs across West and North Texas…"

That answers *what*, *who*, *where*, and carries a safety stance, in two
sentences. Contrast with the original homepage hero, which opened with
"Premium Garage Doors. Built to last" — true, but it answers nothing.

## Internal linking

The site now has a real pillar/cluster structure, which it did not have when
the original audit ran (2 pages, nav-and-footer links only):

```
/services (hub)
  ├── /services/residential-garage-doors ──┐
  ├── /services/commercial-overhead-doors ─┤── cross-link to each other
  ├── /services/garage-door-repair ────────┤   ("Other services")
  └── /services/garage-door-spring-repair ─┘
        │
        └── "Related reading" → matching /blog/<slug> posts

/service-areas (hub) → 15 city pages → /#contact
/blog (hub) → 9 posts → related posts + contact CTA
```

- Every service page links to 3 others plus 2–3 topically matched blog posts
- Blog posts link to siblings via `related`
- Footer links every service page from every page on the site
- **Orphans:** none. Every page is reachable from the nav or footer.

⚠️ **Remaining weakness:** city pages don't link to service pages. A visitor on
`/service-areas/vernon` who needs a spring fixed has no direct path to
`/services/garage-door-spring-repair`. Flagged to the action plan — it's the
highest-value internal-linking fix left.

## Images

- All content images carry descriptive alt text — not filename dumps, and not
  the bare title string (which was the original defect on service cards)
- Hero is `fetchpriority="high"` and preloaded from `<head>` with the hashed
  URL discovered at build time
- Gallery images are lazy + `decoding="async"`
- Per-post 1200×630 OG cards generated at build from each post's hero photo

⚠️ Gallery `<img>` tags still lack intrinsic `width`/`height`, which is a CLS
risk inside the masonry layout on `/work`. Carried forward from the original
audit; still open.

**One correction made this pass:** the `/about` photo alt originally described
Colten installing a door. The image is actually a close-up of hinge and roller
hardware. Alt text has to describe what's in the frame — fixed.
